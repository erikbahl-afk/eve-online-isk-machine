## Lessons
1. **Step-downs sell stock, but fees add up.** 45 relists this week cost 41M, 38M of it on Amarr legacy orders. Realized
   slid from −35.5M (Sun) to −47.1M on relist fees alone. Each 10% step costs about 1.4% of the order's value: worth it for
   the 2.4B still stuck, not as a routine.
2. **"Kept" isn't the same as "working".** The bid check asked whether a bid was still worth having, not whether it could
   ever reach the front of the queue. Ranged Perimeter bids (seen since Wed) made the queue honest and showed the gap.
3. **The 1B twins flipped again.** The extra-time lead (+4.5% last week) went to −0.13% vs +2.45%. Noise so far, so we
   can't yet say what extra login time is worth.
4. **The open-question experiments give no signal.** Exports and rotation made no new import or export trips this week,
   so there's nothing to judge them on. Seeding was killed Thu for the same reason.
