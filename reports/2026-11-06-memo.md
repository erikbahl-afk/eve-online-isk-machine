## Lessons
1. **The picker likes thin, wide-spread items, and that's a trap on both sides.** Imports bought SKINs, clothing and a Standup
   rig whose Amarr bids are 0–7% of cost (fixed Thursday: SKIN/Apparel excluded, `min_exit_frac` 0.6).
2. **Station bids on rare items don't fill.** 7 bids (1.64B, 34% of net worth) placed 31 Oct have 0 fills in 6 days; 4 are
   now outbid. In our own snapshots, their "bid-side trades" were mostly 0.8-unit events, i.e. one vanished buy order
   counted at `gone_weight` 0.8. A cancelled order was being read as a sale. Official 30-day history: 1–4 trades/day in
   the whole region, at prices close to the ask.
3. **Broker fees are paid up front on the full bid,** so a bid that never fills is a pure loss (no_imports: 71M paid on 3% fills).
4. Alerts worked: all 9 open alerts pointed at real problems.

## Step-back (first Friday)
- **Fill share:** `alpha` 0.6 is not the problem; inferring trades from vanished orders is, on items with 1–3 orders per side.
  Sunday's fills session should require ≥2 *distinct* bid events (or official volume) before a station bid counts as fillable.
- **Fees:** fine for liquid items; ruinous for bids that sit. A fill-probability-weighted fee belongs in scoring.
- **What would make this fail:** (a) inferred volume overstated on thin items → capital frozen in bids and stock;
  (b) too few logins to reprice; (c) imports that only sell at the ask in a market with 0.3 sales/day.
- **Alternatives:** manufacturing and a second character stay parked until go-live, as agreed.
- **Go-live readiness: not ready.** No completed buy→sell round trip yet. The earliest go-live is late Nov / early Dec
  (Career Program SP). We need ≥1 week of real sales in practice before then.
