## Lessons
1. **Step-downs do clear big legacy stock.** One 10% cut on the repairer sold 2 units within 4 days. The Typhoon SKIN
   (418M, 8.2%) is on the same path. No dump.
2. **A change to the saved state needs a test that loads the old format.** Tuesday's new columns broke every run for
   24 h when the day rolled over. The test is in place now.
3. **The bottleneck is capital allocation, not the model.** Station bids tie up the ISK that would fund the leg that
   actually earns. The 5-day bid cancel rarely fires on time: logins come about 3.5 times a week, so a bid usually sits 6–7
   days before a session sees it as stale. The Mon bids would have turned 5 days old 4 minutes after Sat's login.
4. **The time-on-top floor holds out of sample** (Tue refit f 0.28 vs 0.33), but fill_floor still trails on fees.
   Paper fills come from trade prints, so it only changes which items get picked.
5. **Experiments that can't trigger tell us nothing.** Exports and rotation were killed for that reason. The new ones
   have dated success measures (imports_only by 12-10: at least 1 courier a week, beating main on return and realized).
